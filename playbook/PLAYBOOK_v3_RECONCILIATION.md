## The one genuine TEMPLATE DEFECT — resolved by adopted internal default (counsel to confirm + redraft)

**#3 — uNDA confidentiality survival is open-ended/ambiguous.**
- uNDA **Clause 4**: obligations apply "… **up to the time period prescribed in Clause 8**."
- uNDA **Clause 8**: "… each Party's rights and obligations … **will survive any termination or expiration of this Agreement**." — **no period is prescribed.**
- So Clause 4 points to a sunset that Clause 8 never sets → uNDA confidentiality effectively survives **indefinitely** (or is ambiguous). Present in both `uNDA_2.2_Service Provider` and `uNDA_3.3_Individual`.
- Contrast: the **mNDA Clause 8 is clean** (2 years from Effective Date).
- **Recommendation for counsel — ADOPTED internal default (5-Opus panel, pending counsel refinement):** add a finite sunset to uNDA Clause 8 — **5 years per disclosure for ordinary CI + an uncapped trade-secret tail, measured from each disclosure, with a 3-year pre-authorized fallback** (paste-ready redraft in the panel section below). This supersedes the earlier placeholder of mirroring the mNDA's 2-year-from-Effective-Date tail, which the panel expressly considered and rejected for a one-way discloser of long-lived hardware IP.

## Net adopted standard (template-true) — corrections to the playbook defaults

- **Term:** 1 year from the Effective Date; either party may terminate earlier on written notice.
- **Survival (mNDA):** 2 years from the Effective Date; trade secrets perpetual (while they remain trade secrets).
- **Survival (uNDA):** ADOPTED internal default (5-Opus panel) — **5 years per disclosure for ordinary CI + uncapped trade-secret tail, measured per disclosure, 3-year pre-authorized fallback**; pending counsel refinement (paste-ready Clause 8 redraft in the panel section below; `defaults.uNDA_survival_recommendation`). The earlier "2 years from Effective Date to match mNDA" was a placeholder the panel rejected.
- **Exclusions (5):** prior possession · public domain · third-party receipt · independent development · approved-for-release by written authorization.

- **Governing law / forum:** US/Amperesand Inc → Delaware law + AAA, single arbitrator, **seat Nevada**; SG/Amperesand Pte Ltd → Singapore law + SIAC, single arbitrator, seat Singapore; both with an injunctive-relief carve-out to any court (Clause 12).

## uNDA Clause 8 sunset — 5-Opus panel recommendation (pending counsel)

**Consensus (confidence: high):**
- **Ordinary (non-trade-secret) CI:** Five (5) years for ordinary (non-trade-secret) Confidential Information, plus an uncapped trade-secret tail. Three of five lenses (discloser-protection, enforceability, empirical) land on 5 years as the disclosing-party-favorable end of the enforceable band; the empirical lens shows 5y matches the eval answer-key "correct" language for one-way/discloser-favorable cases, and the enforceability lens confirms 5y is comfortably inside the "reasonable duration" zone courts enforce. The counterparty lens recommends 3y and the template-consistency lens recommends 2y, but both expressly concede a longer fixed tail is defensible and reflect priorities (redline friction, mNDA symmetry) that are subordinate to Amperesand's actual posture as a one-way discloser of long-lived hardware IP. Counsel adopts 5 years as the default with a pre-authorized fallback to 3 years for resistant counterparties.
- **Measurement basis:** From the date of each disclosure (rolling, per-item), NOT from the Effective Date. This is unanimous across all five lenses. The Term is only 1 year but disclosures flow continuously throughout (and during) the services relationship; an Effective-Date anchor (as the mNDA uses) would shrink protection on the latest, often most sensitive, disclosures to weeks. Per-disclosure measurement gives every disclosure a uniform full window, matches Amperesand's own seed template house style ("date of disclosure"), and is the most defensible basis against a duration/reasonableness challenge because each item's clock is tethered to its own disclosure rather than an open-ended agreement term. The trade-secret tier is not measured by a fixed clock at all; it runs by reference to the information's continuing status as a trade secret.
- **Trade secrets:** Bifurcate explicitly into two tiers (unanimous across all five lenses). Tier (a): non-trade-secret Confidential Information is protected for the fixed 5-year per-disclosure tail, which caps ordinary CI so it cannot be attacked as an unreasonable/perpetual restraint. Tier (b): any Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law continues to be protected for as long as it remains a trade secret (potentially indefinite, but self-limiting if the information loses trade-secret status). Key safeguards on which the panel agrees: (1) key the carve-out to "applicable law" rather than a single statute so it travels across Delaware, Nevada, and Singapore (DTSA / applicable UTSA / Singapore equitable "quality of confidence"); (2) define "Trade Secret" by reference to the governing statute/doctrine, not Amperesand's unilateral labeling, to avoid an overbreadth finding that taints the clause; (3) make clear expiry of the 5-year tier is not a waiver of the trade-secret tier; (4) Amperesand must maintain reasonable secrecy measures (marking, access controls) or the trade-secret tail collapses to the 5-year floor. Optional belt-and-suspenders (counsel decision): give trade-secret items the 5-year tier as a floor in addition to the indefinite tier, so loss of trade-secret status never leaves an item with less than the ordinary-CI protection.

**Recommended Clause 8 redraft (paste-ready):**

>8. Term and Survival. The term of this Agreement (the "Term") shall commence on the Effective Date and shall expire one (1) year from the Effective Date, unless earlier terminated by either Party upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice. Notwithstanding any termination or expiration of this Agreement, each Party's rights and obligations under this Agreement that by their nature are intended to survive (including, without limitation, the confidentiality and non-use obligations set forth in Clause 4) shall survive as follows: (a) with respect to any Confidential Information that does not constitute a Trade Secret, the Receiving Party's confidentiality and non-use obligations shall survive and continue in full force and effect, with respect to each item of such Confidential Information, for a period of five (5) years from the date such item was disclosed to the Receiving Party; and (b) with respect to any Confidential Information that constitutes a Trade Secret, the Receiving Party's confidentiality and non-use obligations shall survive and continue for so long as such information continues to constitute a Trade Secret under applicable law, which obligation shall survive and is not limited by, and shall not be waived by the expiration of, the period set forth in clause (a). For purposes of this Agreement, "Trade Secret" means any Confidential Information that qualifies for protection as a trade secret under applicable law, including the U.S. Defend Trade Secrets Act, any applicable U.S. state Uniform Trade Secrets Act (including those of Delaware and Nevada), and, where applicable, the laws of Singapore. The "time period prescribed in Clause 8" referenced in Clause 4 means, as applicable, the five (5) year period in clause (a) above or, with respect to Trade Secrets, the period in clause (b) above. For the avoidance of doubt, expiration or termination of the Term shall not relieve the Receiving Party of any obligation accrued, or in respect of any breach occurring, prior to such expiration or termination, nor of its obligations to return or destroy Confidential Information in accordance with this Agreement.

**Points of disagreement among the panel:**
- Length of the ordinary-CI tier: 5 years (discloser, enforceability, empirical lenses) vs 3 years (counterparty lens) vs 2 years to mirror the mNDA (template-consistency lens). Resolved in favor of 5 years with a pre-authorized 3-year fallback.
- Whether to mirror the mNDA exactly: template-consistency lens favors structural mirroring (2y) for suite harmony; the majority treats the mNDA's 2y as a bilateral artifact that does not fit a one-way discloser and justifies divergence.
- Belt-and-suspenders floor for trade secrets: template-consistency lens flags that if trade-secret status is lost after the fixed window runs, an item could fall to zero protection and suggests giving trade secrets at least the fixed period as a floor; other lenses leave the two tiers cleanly separate. Flagged as a counsel decision.
- Adding a 30-day early-termination right: included by several lenses as a market-expected friction-reducer, but template-consistency lens warns it is a change beyond the stated defect and should be removed if the template intends a non-terminable 1-year term.
- Enumerating other surviving clauses by number (e.g., return/destruction, remedies, IP, governing law): the enforceability lens lists them explicitly; others fold this into a general 'by their nature survive' phrase. Bracketed cross-references must be conformed to actual uNDA numbering if used.

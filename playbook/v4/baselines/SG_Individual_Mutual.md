NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, terms and conditions set forth herein, the Parties agree as follows:
1.	CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION. Except as set forth in Clause 2 below, “Confidential Information” shall mean any and all information relating to the Purpose, disclosed, furnished or communicated by or on behalf of a Party (the “Disclosing Party”) to the other Party (the “Receiving Party”), which is marked as “confidential” or communicated to be confidential. Such Confidential Information may include, without limitation (a) such information which is confidential or proprietary to the Disclosing Party relating to, among others, (i) the business, affairs, customers, clients, suppliers, plans, intentions, or market opportunities of the Disclosing Party and/ or any of its existing or future subsidiaries contemplated to be established (to the extent disclosed); and/or (ii) the intellectual property, operations, processes, product information, know-how, technical information, designs, trade secrets, or software of the Disclosing Party; (b) any information, findings, data or analysis derived from the Confidential Information; (c) any shared personal data; and (d) any other information that is identified as being of a confidential or proprietary nature at the time of disclosure, including but not limited to, confidential information of third parties disclosed by them to the Disclosing Party. Notwithstanding the foregoing, regardless of whether the Confidential Information was identified or marked as confidential, if it is reasonably apparent to someone familiar with the subject area that such information is the type typically considered confidential by others in the industry or if the Receiving Party has reason to believe that the information received may be Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall nevertheless treat such confidential information as the Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party.
2.	EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION. The Receiving Party will not have any obligations under this Agreement with respect to any Confidential Information which:
is already in the possession of or known to the Receiving Party or its Representatives (as defined under Clause 4) prior to disclosure to the Receiving Party by the Disclosing Party;
is in or comes into the public domain through no fault or breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party or its Representatives;
is received by the Receiving Party or its Representatives from a third party who is not known by the Receiving Party to be under any obligation of confidentiality in disclosing the same;
is independently developed by the Receiving Party or its Representatives, whether on its own or jointly with a third party or third parties, without regard or reference to any Confidential Information; or
is approved for release or use by written authorization of the Disclosing Party.
3.	REQUIRED DISCLOSURE. In the event that the Receiving Party is obligated or requested to disclose any Confidential Information as a result of a valid order of a court or any governmental body or regulatory authority having jurisdiction, the Receiving Party, or the Representative (as defined under Clause 4) so obligated or requested to disclose the Confidential Information, shall (i) to the extent not legally prohibited from doing do, provide the Disclosing Party with reasonable prior written notice of such disclosure, (ii) disclose only such Confidential Information to the extent required by the relevant court order or governmental or regulatory authority, and (iii) use reasonable efforts to ensure that such Confidential Information disclosed will be accorded confidential treatment. If the Receiving Party is unable to notify the Disclosing Party before Confidential Information is disclosed pursuant to this Clause 3, it shall, to the extent permitted by law, inform the Disclosing Party of the full circumstances of the disclosure and the information that has been disclosed as soon as reasonably practicable after such disclosure has been made.
4.	CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS. Subject to Clause 2, the Receiving Party agrees that at all times and notwithstanding any termination or expiration of this Agreement, up to the time period prescribed in Clause 8, it will hold in strict confidence and not disclose to any third party any Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party, except as approved in writing by the Disclosing Party. The Receiving Party agrees to use at least the same degree of precaution as it would use to protect its own confidential information of like importance but in no event less than reasonable care. The Receiving Party will limit access to the Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party to only those directors, officers, employees or advisers of the Receiving Party whose duties require them to possess or consider the Confidential Information, each strictly on a “need to know” basis only (such persons or entities, collectively, “Representatives” and each a “Representative”). The Receiving Party acknowledges and agrees that it shall be responsible for any breach of the terms of this Agreement by any of its Representatives and shall take all reasonable measures to restrain such Representatives from prohibited or unauthorized disclosure or use of the Confidential Information. The Receiving Party will promptly notify the Disclosing Party upon discovery of any loss or unauthorized disclosure of the Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party.
5.	NO PUBLICITY. No public release or announcement, whether oral or in writing, relating to the Purpose or any matter contemplated under this Agreement shall be made by the Receiving Party unless agreed in writing by the Disclosing Party and only at such time and in such form as is approved by the Disclosing Party.
6.	NO OTHER OBLIGATIONS. For the avoidance of doubt, nothing contained herein shall compel or oblige: (i) the Disclosing Party to supply the Receiving Party with any or all Confidential Information requested by the Receiving Party (and Disclosing Party shall be entitled at its discretion to decline to supply the Receiving Party with any part of such information); or (ii) either or both Parties to enter into any transaction in connection with the Purpose or otherwise.
7.	RETURN OR DESTRUCTION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION. Within fourteen (14) days of (i) receipt of written request by the Disclosing Party in accordance with Clause 9, or (ii) expiration or termination of this Agreement in accordance with Clause 8, the Receiving Party will return to the Disclosing Party or destroy all documents and other tangible materials representing the Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information and all copies thereof, provided that the Receiving Party shall be permitted to retain one (1) copy of Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information (including such copies of any electronic records and files containing any such Confidential Information which have been created pursuant to its automatic archiving and back-up procedures) for the sole purpose of performing its surviving obligations hereunder and for so long as required by any law, court or regulatory agency or authority or its internal audit or internal compliance procedures. The provisions of this Agreement shall continue to apply to any documents and materials kept by the Receiving Party in accordance with this Clause 7.
8.	TERM AND TERMINATION. The term of this Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall expire one (1) year from the Effective Date, provided that either Party may terminate this Agreement upon written notice to the other Party in accordance with Clause 9. Notwithstanding anything to the contrary herein, each Party’s rights and obligations under this Agreement will survive any termination or expiration of this Agreement for a period of two (2) years from the Effective Date; provided, however, any information which constitutes trade secrets of the Disclosing Party shall continue to be deemed Confidential Information subject to the provisions of this Agreement for as long as such information constitutes trade secrets.

No modification of or amendment to this Agreement will be effective unless in writing and signed by authorized representatives of the Parties.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, terms and conditions set forth herein, the Parties agree as follows:
1.	CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION. Except as set forth in Clause 2 below, “Confidential Information” shall mean any and all information relating to the Purpose, disclosed, furnished or communicated, whether in writing or orally, by or on behalf of the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party or to an authorized third party acting on behalf of the Receiving Party, which is marked as “confidential” or communicated as such. Such Confidential Information may include, without limitation (a) the fact that this Agreement exists or that discussions and negotiations are taking place concerning the Purpose and the status of those discussions and negotiations; (b) the terms and conditions of this Agreement; (c) such information which is confidential or proprietary to the Disclosing Party relating to, among others, (i) the business, affairs, customers, clients, suppliers, plans, intentions, or market opportunities of the Disclosing Party, its Affiliates and/ or any of its existing or subsidiaries contemplated to be established; and/or (ii) the intellectual property, operations, processes, product information, know-how, technical information, designs, trade secrets, or software of the Disclosing Party, its Affiliates and/ or any of its future portfolio companies contemplated to be established; (d) any information, findings, data or analysis derived from the Confidential Information; (e) any shared personal data; (f) any confidential information of third parties disclosed by them to the Disclosing Party and (g) any other information that is identified as being of a confidential or proprietary nature. Notwithstanding the foregoing, regardless of whether the Confidential Information was identified or marked as confidential, if it is reasonably apparent to someone familiar with the subject area that such information is the type typically considered confidential by others in the industry or if the Receiving Party has reason to believe that the information received may be Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall nevertheless treat such confidential information as the Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party. For the purpose of this Agreement, “Affiliate” shall mean, with respect to a particular person or entity, another person or entity that, directly or indirectly, controls, is controlled by or is under common control with such person or entity, as the case may be.
2.	EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION. The Receiving Party will not have any obligations under this Agreement with respect to any Confidential Information which:
is already in the possession of or known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure to the Receiving Party by the Disclosing Party;
is in or comes into the public domain through no fault or breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party or its Representatives (as defined under Clause 4);
is received by the Receiving Party from a third party who is not under any obligation of confidentiality in disclosing the same;
is independently developed by the Receiving Party without regard or reference to any Confidential Information; or
is approved for release or use by written authorization of the Disclosing Party.
3.	REQUIRED DISCLOSURE. In the event that the Receiving Party is obligated or requested to disclose any Confidential Information as a result of a valid order of a court or any governmental body or regulatory authority having jurisdiction, the Receiving Party, or the Representative (as defined under Clause 4) so obligated or requested to disclose the Confidential Information, shall (i) provide the Disclosing Party with reasonable prior written notice of such disclosure, (ii) disclose only such Confidential Information to the extent required by the relevant court order or governmental or regulatory authority, and (iii) use reasonable efforts to ensure that such Confidential Information disclosed will be accorded confidential treatment. If the Receiving Party is unable to notify the Disclosing Party before Confidential Information is disclosed pursuant to this Clause 3, it shall, to the extent permitted by law, inform the Disclosing Party of the full circumstances of the disclosure and the information that has been disclosed as soon as reasonably practicable after such disclosure has been made.
4.	CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS. Subject to Clause 2, the Receiving Party agrees that at all times and notwithstanding any termination or expiration of this Agreement, up to the time period prescribed in Clause 8, it will hold in strict confidence and not disclose to any third party any Confidential Information, except as approved in writing by the Disclosing Party, and will use the Confidential Information for no purpose other than the Purpose. The Receiving Party agrees to use at least the same degree of precaution as it would use to protect its own confidential information of like importance but in no event less than reasonable care. The Receiving Party will limit access to the Confidential Information to only those of the Receiving Party’s directors, officers, employees, consultants or advisers whose duties require them to possess or consider the Confidential Information, each strictly on a “need to know” basis only (such persons or entities, collectively, “Representatives” and each a “Representative”). The Receiving Party acknowledges and agrees that it shall be responsible for any breach of the terms of this Agreement by any of its Representatives and shall take all reasonable measures (including but not limited to court proceedings) to restrain such Representatives from prohibited or unauthorized disclosure or use of the Confidential Information. The Receiving Party will not copy, reduce to writing or otherwise record the Confidential Information except as strictly necessary for the Purpose. Any such copies, reductions to writing and records shall be the property of the Disclosing Party. The Receiving Party will immediately notify the Disclosing Party upon discovery of any loss or unauthorized disclosure of the Confidential Information.
5.	NO PUBLICITY. No public release or announcement, whether oral or in writing, relating to the Purpose or any matter contemplated under this Agreement shall be made by the Receiving Party unless agreed in writing by the Disclosing Party and only at such time and in such form as is approved by the Disclosing Party.
6.	NO OTHER OBLIGATIONS. For the avoidance of doubt, nothing contained herein shall compel or oblige the Disclosing Party to (i) supply the Receiving Party with any or all Confidential Information requested by the Receiving Party (and Disclosing Party shall be entitled at its discretion to decline to supply the Receiving Party with any part of such information); or (ii) enter into any transaction in connection with the Purpose or otherwise. Nothing in this Agreement is intended to, or shall be deemed to, establish any partnership or joint venture between the Parties, constitute any Party the agent of the other Party, or authorize any Party to make or enter into any commitments for or on behalf of any other Party. Either Party may at any time, in its sole discretion with or without cause, terminate discussions and negotiations with the other Party, in connection with the Purpose or otherwise.
7.	RETURN OR DESTRUCTION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION. Within fourteen (14) days of (i) receipt of written request by the Disclosing Party in accordance with Clause 9, or (ii) expiration or termination of this Agreement in accordance with Clause 8, the Receiving Party will return to the Disclosing Party or destroy all documents and other tangible materials representing the Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information and all copies thereof, provided that the Receiving Party shall be permitted to retain one (1) copy of Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information (including such copies of any electronic records and files containing any such Confidential Information which have been created pursuant to its automatic archiving and back-up procedures) for the sole purpose of performing its surviving obligations hereunder and for so long as required by any law, court or regulatory agency or authority or its internal audit or internal compliance procedures. The provisions of this Agreement shall continue to apply to any documents and materials kept by the Receiving Party in accordance with this Clause 7.
